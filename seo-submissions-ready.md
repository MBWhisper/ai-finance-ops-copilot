• Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) — Automatic LTV calculations and LTV:CAC ratio monitoring
• Cash Flow Forecasting — 90-day cash flow projections based on current revenue and burn rate
• Runway Calculator — Know exactly how many months of cash you have left
• AI Copilot — Ask financial questions in plain English and get instant, actionable answers
• Benchmark Reports — Compare your metrics against 2026 SaaS industry benchmarks by stage

Who It's For:
Solo SaaS founders, bootstrapped teams, and seed-stage startups who need financial clarity without hiring a CFO or paying for expensive analytics tools.

AI Finance Ops is free for solo founders with no MRR limits. Premium plans available for teams.
