Stripe now has a surprisingly capable analytics dashboard. So is Baremetrics still worth $150-$400/month? The answer depends on what stage you're at and what you actually need.

For early-stage founders, the question is real: every dollar matters, and adding another subscription feels wasteful if your existing tools already "work." But as your subscription base grows, the gap between what Stripe shows you and what you need to know becomes a liability. This comparison breaks down exactly where each tool excels and where you might need something more.

## What Stripe's Native Analytics Gives You

In 2026, Stripe's built-in dashboard provides a solid foundation for subscription analytics:

- **Revenue overview** — gross revenue, net revenue, refunds, and disputes
- **MRR and ARR** — visible in the dashboard for subscription products
- **Churn** — customer and revenue churn visible under the Subscriptions tab
- **Active subscribers** — count and trend over time
- **Failed payment recovery** — Smart Retries stats and recovery rates
- **Cohort analysis** — basic retention cohorts by signup month
- **Revenue by customer** — individual customer payment history
- **Payment method breakdown** — card types, failure reasons

For a founder at $0–$5K MRR with a single product and one or two pricing tiers, this is often enough. Stripe gives you the raw data you need to understand what's happening in your business.

The catch is in the word "raw." Stripe shows you the numbers, but it doesn't always make them easy to interpret. MRR decomposition — the breakdown of how much came from new customers versus expansion versus churn — requires you to piece together data from multiple tabs. And forecasting? You're building your own spreadsheets.

## What Baremetrics Adds on Top

Baremetrics was built specifically for SaaS analytics on top of Stripe. It pulls your Stripe data and transforms it into metrics that matter for subscription businesses:

| Feature | Stripe Native | Baremetrics |
|---|---|---|
| MRR decomposition (new/expansion/churn) | Partial | Yes |
| LTV calculation | No | Yes |
| Forecast / runway | No | Yes |
| Customer-level metrics | Basic | Deep |
| Slack / email digests | No | Yes |
| Trial conversion tracking | No | Yes |
| QuickBooks/Xero integration | No | Yes |
| Revenue dashboards for non-technical stakeholders | No | Yes |
| Smart alerts (churn spikes, revenue drops) | No | Yes |
| Price | Free (with Stripe) | $129–$399/month |

The feature gap is real. Baremetrics gives you metrics that Stripe simply doesn't compute — like LTV (lifetime value), quick ratio, and runway projections based on current growth trends.

## The Real Difference: Actionability

Stripe tells you *what happened*. Baremetrics helps you understand *why* and *what to do next*.

**Example:** Stripe shows your MRR dropped by $2,000 this month. That's useful information, but it doesn't tell you the story behind it.

Baremetrics breaks it down: 4 customers churned (−$3,200) but 2 existing customers upgraded (+$1,200), and you acquired 3 new customers (+$800). Net change: −$1,200 — not −$2,000. The "real" MRR drop is smaller than the headline number, and the causes are completely different.

That distinction matters. If you only saw the Stripe number, you might panic and overhaul your acquisition strategy. The Baremetrics breakdown tells you the real problem is churn, not acquisition — a completely different operational response.

### Where Stripe's Dashboard Falls Short

1. **No unified MRR decomposition.** You have to manually separate new, expansion, contraction, and churned MRR from multiple Stripe views.

2. **No LTV or CAC.** Stripe doesn't calculate customer lifetime value or customer acquisition cost. These are foundational metrics for any subscription business.

3. **Limited forecasting.** Stripe shows you historical trends but doesn't project forward. You can't see your runway or forecast next quarter's revenue without exporting data and building your own model.

4. **No automated reporting.** There's no Slack digest, no weekly email summary. You have to log into Stripe and navigate to the right tab to see your numbers.

5. **Basic cohort analysis.** Stripe's retention cohorts are functional but lack the granularity that Baremetrics provides — like filtering by plan, acquisition channel, or billing interval.

## When Stripe Native Is Enough

Stripe's built-in analytics are sufficient when:

- You're under $10K MRR with a simple subscription model
- You have one product and one or two pricing tiers
- You don't need forecasting or runway visibility
- You're the only person reading the numbers
- You're comfortable building your own spreadsheets for deeper analysis

For bootstrapped founders in the earliest stages, Stripe's dashboard gives you the essentials without adding another tool to manage.

## When You Need More Than Stripe

Consider upgrading to Baremetrics (or a similar tool) when:

- You're above $10K MRR and decisions require precise metric decomposition
- You need to share metrics with co-founders, advisors, or investors regularly
- You want automated email/Slack digests without logging into Stripe
- You need LTV, CAC, or runway calculations
- You're running multiple pricing tiers and need to understand which segments drive growth
- You're preparing for a fundraise and need polished, investor-ready metrics

The transition usually happens around $5K–$15K MRR. Below that, Stripe works fine. Above it, the time you spend wrestling with data exports starts to cost more than a Baremetrics subscription.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Baremetrics worth it over Stripe's free analytics?

It depends on your stage. Below $5K MRR with a simple pricing model, Stripe's built-in analytics are usually sufficient. Above $5K MRR, Baremetrics saves you hours of manual analysis and provides metrics (LTV, CAC, runway) that Stripe doesn't calculate. If budget is a concern, AI Finance Ops offers the same metrics for free.

### Can I use Baremetrics with payment processors other than Stripe?

No. Baremetrics is built exclusively on Stripe. If you use PayPal, Gumroad, Paddle, or LemonSqueezy, Baremetrics won't pull that data. AI Finance Ops supports manual entry for non-Stripe processors.

### What's the best free alternative to Baremetrics?

AI Finance Ops provides MRR decomposition, LTV, CAC, runway forecasting, and AI insights — all free to start. It connects to Stripe and also supports manual subscriber entry for other processors.

### When should I switch from Stripe's dashboard to a dedicated analytics tool?

The typical trigger is when you're spending more than 2–3 hours per month wrestling with spreadsheets to get the metrics you need. At $5K+ MRR, the time cost of manual analysis usually exceeds the cost of a tool like AI Finance Ops (free) or Baremetrics ($129/month).

### Can I use both Stripe's dashboard and Baremetrics (or AI Finance Ops)?

Yes. Most founders use Stripe's dashboard for payment operations (refunds, disputes, payment failures) and a dedicated analytics tool for subscription metrics (MRR, churn, LTV). They serve different purposes.
