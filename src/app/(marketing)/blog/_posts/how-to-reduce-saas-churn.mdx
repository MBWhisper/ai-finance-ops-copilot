Churn is the fastest way to kill growth — even with great acquisition.

Five tactics:
1. Fix onboarding (time-to-value under 24h)
2. Add exit surveys (learn why they leave)
3. Offer annual plans (improves cash + retention)
4. Build usage triggers (alert at-risk accounts before cancellation)
5. Offer a downgrade path (keep the account instead of losing it)
