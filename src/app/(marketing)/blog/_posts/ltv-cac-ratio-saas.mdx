LTV:CAC is the single best health check for SaaS unit economics.

## Formula

**LTV = (ARPU × Gross Margin) / Churn Rate**
**CAC = Sales + Marketing / New Customers**
**Ratio = LTV / CAC**

## 2026 Benchmarks

| Stage | Good Ratio |
|---|---|
| Pre-seed | 2:1+ |
| Seed | 3:1+ |
| Series A+ | 3:1 to 5:1 |

If your ratio is below 3:1, improve activation or reduce CAC by focusing on organic channels.
