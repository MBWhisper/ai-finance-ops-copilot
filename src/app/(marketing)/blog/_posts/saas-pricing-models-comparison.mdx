Per-seat: predictable, scales with team size
Usage-based: scales with value, unpredictable for forecasting
Flat-rate: simple, leaves money on the table
Hybrid: best for most B2B SaaS (base + usage overage)

The mistake most founders make: setting price once and never revisiting. Test increases with new cohorts.
