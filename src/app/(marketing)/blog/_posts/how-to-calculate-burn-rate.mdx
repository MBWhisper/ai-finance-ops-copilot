Burn rate tells you how fast you are spending cash before revenue covers it.

## The Formula

**Net Burn = Total Expenses - Revenue**
**Gross Burn = Total Expenses (before revenue)**
**Burn Multiple = Net Burn / Net New ARR**

## 2026 Benchmarks

| Stage | Good Burn Multiple |
|---|---|
| Pre-seed | < 3.0 |
| Seed | 1.5 - 2.5 |
| Series A | 1.0 - 1.5 |

If your burn multiple is above 3, cut non-essential tools or raise a bridge round.
